[Ask about a scoped pilot](mailto:gerardrecinto@gmail.com?subject=genai-gateway%3A%20scoped%20paid%20help%20inquiry). Describe your environment and the outcome you need. Share only redacted examples.

Scope, price, timing, and any code-use permissions need agreement before work begins. This inquiry route does not imply a hosted service, response deadline, or production certification.
